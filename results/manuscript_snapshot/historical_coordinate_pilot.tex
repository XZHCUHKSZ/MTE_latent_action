\paragraph{Controlled coordinate and propagation pilot.}
\label{app:coordinatepilot}
Within each frozen Simple, Graph, Tree or MIF class, the control
replaces only M\"obius/inverse-zeta buffers with identities, retaining
the complete input information and equal allocated parameters.
Simple/Graph/Tree use h3 edges; MIF uses the three-horizon
endpoint-plus-edge interface. Separate MIF controls replace one time,
coalition or entity propagation matrix; Tree's upward-only switch
reduces active computation.
This historical two-seed development design comprises 12 configurations and 48 tasks; its MPE rows predate the two-step protocol and are not new control evidence.
Each has 400 updates, batch 64, learning rate $10^{-3}$, hidden width
128, latent width 16 and at most 2,048 fitting/256 development
transitions. Paired arms share initialization, samples and optimizer.
The objective is value-coordinate reconstruction MSE, variance floor,
and $0.02$ times off-diagonal covariance penalty; no extra coefficient
loss. This symmetric pilot differs from the formal masked objectives.
It reports learned coefficient reconstruction, linear readout and
whole-edge error; it trains no history policy or decoder.

In this historical pilot, Simple/Graph coordinate effects are positive in both environments
and both pilot seeds; MPE Tree also benefits. MaMuJoCo Tree's absolute
skill is negative, and MIF shows no consistent coordinate or axis
benefit. The pilot tests architecture-specific compression, without
establishing convergence or MIF-Aux downstream attribution.
